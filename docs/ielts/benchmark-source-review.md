# IELTS benchmark source review

This ledger records source-level release decisions for the protected IELTS
grading benchmark. It is not a corpus manifest and contains no learner
responses or gold labels. The protected importer remains the enforcement point.

## Acceptance rule

A grading-authoritative source needs all of the following:

- official IELTS or qualified-human-examiner provenance;
- commercial-use-compatible rights, or explicit written permission;
- the actual response artifact and all four criterion scores;
- a stable source locator and checksum;
- privacy/de-identification review;
- one source-level split, so related examples cannot leak across development
  and evaluation.

Holistic scores, CEFR conversions, AI-generated labels, marketing claims, and
descriptions of unavailable data are insufficient.

## 2026-09-02 public-source sweep

Exa discovery reviewed 210 result slots across four independent workstreams and
deep-read 18 shortlisted official, research, licensing, and dataset pages. The
count is search results reviewed, not a claim that every page on the public
internet was exhausted. No public source met the commercial rights, complete
criterion-label, source-isolation, audio, double-marking, and adjudication
requirements together.

- The official IELTS score-setting resource publishes 11 Speaking samples from
  overall Band 5 through Band 9, with transcripts and examiner comments covering
  all four criteria. It does not publish four numeric criterion scores. DebateLab
  may register derived, answer-free locators as coaching candidates, but must not
  use them as protected criterion-label truth.
- The official Academic and General Training Writing sample PDFs publish six
  currently verified scored responses (overall Bands 4, 5, 5.5, 6, and 7.5)
  with examiner comments. They likewise provide an overall task band rather than
  four numeric criterion labels. Only derived locators enter the v4 candidate
  package; no response text is copied into the corpus.
- No publicly discoverable Speaking corpus met all of the product gate at once:
  commercially usable rights, audio, prompt, all four human IELTS criterion
  scores, and accent metadata.
- Several discoverable Kaggle and Hugging Face collections describe
  criterion-level labels generated by GPT, Gemini, or other models. Those labels
  are explicitly excluded from calibration and benchmark ground truth.
- IELTS's current copyright statement covers text, images, photos, and sound and
  limits ordinary site-material use to personal, non-commercial purposes.
  Public availability therefore does not clear official samples for a
  commercial retrieval corpus or benchmark. Written permission remains a hard
  source-level requirement.
- The 2023 official Writing descriptors provide two narrow deterministic
  insufficiency rules: a genuine non-attempt is Band 0, and a response of 20
  words or fewer is Band 1. Bands 2 and 3 remain qualitative judgements and must
  not be inferred from length alone. For Speaking, even an empty ASR transcript
  is not sufficient proof of Band 0 because recognition may have failed.
- The official Writing-scale validation reports large examiner trials, and the
  pronunciation research confirms that intelligibility, listener effort,
  segmental features, prosody, and accent effects all matter. Neither report
  publishes a commercially licensed case-level criterion benchmark.

The v4 source package remains `needs_review`, `requires_review`, and
coaching-only for scored-example locators. A different human must approve the
source rights and each item before publication; import success is not approval.

## Open release dependencies

- Commission first-party Writing responses with the actual four criterion
  labels and complete their consent/privacy review. Public candidate datasets
  remain ineligible unless their authors provide the missing case-level labels,
  examiner provenance, adjudication record, and commercial rights.
- Obtain a commercially usable Speaking set containing audio, prompt, all four
  human criterion scores, and accent metadata. Public holistic/CEFR corpora do
  not meet this requirement.
- Keep `IELTS_EVIDENCE_ADJUDICATION_ENABLED=false` until the source-separated
  benchmark is imported, evaluated, and passes the pinned release gate.
